Refund and Cancellation Policy – Jyotish Saathi

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Cancelling a subscription

Android app (Google Play): open the Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Jyotish Saathi → Cancel. Website (Razorpay): these payments do not renew by themselves, so there is nothing to cancel. For help, email us at support@jyotishsaathi.com or WhatsApp [YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER] with your registered number. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and you are not charged again.

Refunds

  • Charged by mistake or charged twice: full refund. Write to us within 7 days.
  • Payment taken but Premium did not turn on: write to us with the payment ID. We will turn it on or refund you in full.
  • Subscriptions: you can ask for a full refund within 48 hours of the first payment. After that, we do not refund part-used periods, but you can cancel any time.
  • One-time reports, muhurat and naamkaran lists: these are delivered instantly, so they are not refundable unless they failed to open.
  • Google Play purchases can also be refunded under Google Play's own refund policy.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days.
