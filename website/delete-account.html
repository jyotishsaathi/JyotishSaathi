Delete Your Account – Jyotish Saathi

Last updated: 24 November 2026

You can ask us to delete your Jyotish Saathi account and its data at any time.

How to ask

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to [YOUR WHATSAPP NUMBER] or an email to support@jyotishsaathi.com from your registered number, with the words "Delete my account".
  2. We confirm by replying to that number, then delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your name, WhatsApp number, language, WhatsApp-updates consent, Premium and gift details, Parivaar links, and usage limits counted against your number.

What is kept

Payment records (amount, date, payment ID) are kept for up to 8 years because Indian tax law requires it. Anonymous usage counts have no name or number in them.

Data on your phone

Birth details, family members and saved readings are stored only on your phone. Delete them inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove them all.
