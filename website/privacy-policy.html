Privacy Policy – Jyotish Saathi

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Jyotish Saathi ("the app") is published by Sneha Kumari. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

Your name and WhatsApp number

When you start using the app, we ask for your name and WhatsApp number. We use them to:

  • verify your number – you send a short code from your WhatsApp to our WhatsApp number (or we send you a one-time code);
  • create your account so you can use the app and, in future, Premium;
  • if you tick the box to agree, send you daily rashifal, festival reminders and offers on WhatsApp. You can switch this off anytime in the app (Family → Your account) or by replying STOP.

Your name and number are stored securely on our server (hosted by Cloudflare) and messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). We never sell or share your number with anyone else for their own use.

Information you enter

To make a Kundli, you enter a name, gender, date, time and place of birth for yourself and family members. This information is stored only on your device. It is not sent to us or to any server, and we cannot see it.

Palm photos

For the AI palm reading, you take photos of your left and right palms. The photos, together with your birth chart summary, are sent securely (HTTPS) to our server and to our AI provider (Anthropic) only to create your reading. They are processed immediately and are not stored on our server or used for any other purpose. A small thumbnail and your reading are saved only on your phone.

AI marriage match

When you tap "AI marriage reading", the birth details and calculated chart data of the two people you selected are sent to our server and to Anthropic (Claude AI) to write the reading. They are not used to train AI. The reading is cached for up to 7 days so the same request costs nothing, then deleted.

AI Jyotishi and AI Life Report (Premium)

When a Premium member asks the AI Jyotishi a question or creates a Life Report, the selected person's birth chart summary (no photos) and the question are sent to our server and to Anthropic (Claude AI) to write the answer. They are not used to train AI. Answers are saved only on your phone.

Compass (Vastu)

The Vastu compass reads the phone's motion and compass sensors on the phone only. Nothing from the sensors is saved or sent anywhere.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location only to open Google Maps at the temples nearest to you and to show where the Sun is for the Vastu compass check. Your location is never saved and never sent to our servers; it goes only to Google Maps when you tap a temple button. You can refuse and Maps will use its own "near me" search.

The app asks for camera permission only so you can take these photos. You can refuse and choose photos from your gallery instead.

The quick palm reading (without photos) works entirely on your phone.

Kundli PDF

When you download your Kundli as a PDF, it is created on your phone. It is shared only with the app you choose, for example WhatsApp or Files.

Read-aloud

The "Listen" feature uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech engine.

Sharing

When you tap "Share on WhatsApp", the app opens WhatsApp with your daily reading as a text message. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send it.

Data we collect

Apart from your name and WhatsApp number (above), we do not collect any personal data. Birth details stay on your phone. The app has no advertising and no analytics.

Deleting your data

You can delete any family member inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all details saved on the phone. To delete your name and number from our server, email us at the address below and we will delete them within 30 days.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If future versions add features such as accounts or paid subscriptions, this policy will be updated before those features are released.
